## Inspiration

## story

I first heard the Chimai track in the late 80s in Soviet Union watching a rather
dumb (not then) action movie from France, which was both novel and exciting.
1980s laProfessional never left my body and mind. The dramatic moment described
with sound. The orchestral complexities, beats that evoke almost militaristic
yet fragile state and waves of builds up and anticipation and smoothness.

### Ennio

Ennio Mariconne spent all of his life composing, creating, collaborating in
music and art of films and beyond. Its an inspiring story of a artist that did
not worry that his films started a Spaghetti Westerns in Italy and beyond. He
played a trumpet and piano and had developed a wide range of experience with
electronic and analog instruments.

### My take on this

Loopsessions 3 hour jam ....

I had an opportunity to curate and co-host a Loopsessions event in Yukon, where
diverse music makers create from samples, full tracks, vinyl inspirations and
take 3 hours to showcase whatever came out. Shout out to Jer and Kelvin for
putting in time and creating over 30 of these sessions.
